Your data

Your search profile, letters, evidence bank and practice history are stored only on this device, not on our servers. Studio and Workshop text, and a few still frames from any recording, are sent to our AI provider to generate feedback and aren't kept by Meraki. To prevent misuse, we keep an anonymous usage count per network (a scrambled code, never your IP address), deleted automatically within 30 days. The recording itself stays on your device.
